# The road to Asteru Motion 🎬

I’ve already been making promo videos with animated app screens, music, and wide and portrait versions. Those films show the kind of result I want you to make with Asteru Motion.

**Now I’m getting the app ready for its first beta.** Here’s what I’m bringing together, and where I’d like to take it next.

## 01 · The films are here

**Ready to watch**

The visual direction is already there in the videos I’ve made:

- Real app screens, readable titles, and a story shaped around the app.
- Smooth transitions and animated walkthroughs that give the interface room to breathe.
- Music that supports the pace of the film.
- Wide **16:9** and portrait **9:16** versions for different places to share.
- Light and dark looks, with clean versions the app maker can use.

[Watch the Gitly demo →](https://asteru-motion.asterustudio.workers.dev/#early-look)

## 02 · Your first video in the app

**In development · the first beta**

My next milestone is letting the first app makers create their own videos. The beta will bring this workflow together:

| Part of the beta | What you’ll be able to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Your app, your materials** | Add screenshots and describe what you want people to notice. |
| **A story that fits** | Start with a suggested script or write your own. |
| **Control over the scenes** | Adjust the text, screenshots, order, and timing. |
| **Both formats from the start** | Make a wide **16:9** film or a portrait **9:16** film. |
| **Music included** | Choose music cleared for use in your promo video and adjust its volume. |
| **Preview and export** | Watch the result, make changes, and download a finished MP4. |

**Music and both formats are part of the first beta.** I’m preparing them alongside the editor and export experience.

Before I send invitations, I want someone to be able to go from their screenshots to a finished video, understand what to do next, and keep their work if something goes wrong.

[Join the waitlist →](https://asteru-motion.asterustudio.workers.dev/#join)

## 03 · Make the beta better together

**Next · with the first testers**

I’ll invite a small first group, help with their first videos, and listen to what felt good or got in the way.

- Improve the parts people get stuck on.
- Refine the pacing, text placement, and way app screens are shown.
- Make previewing and exporting more dependable.
- Invite more people as the experience improves.

I’ll share what I change and why in [Updates](https://github.com/asterustudio/asteru-motion-community/discussions/categories/announcements).

## 04 · More ways to tell your story

**After the first beta · shaped by feedback**

| Direction | What I want it to make easier |
| --- | --- |
| **More styles** | Find a look for a quiet product demo, a bold launch, or a feature update. |
| **Start from a link** | Bring in materials from your app’s public page, then review and choose what to use. |
| **Show the app in action** | Add screen recordings, select the useful moments, and mix them with screenshots. |
| **Longer stories** | Give walkthroughs and feature explanations more room, with videos up to three minutes. |
| **More export options** | Choose 60 fps and 4K when your materials and the export service can support them well. |

I’ll choose the order from real requests and what I can deliver reliably. Paid export options will come once I understand the costs and what people find useful in the beta.
