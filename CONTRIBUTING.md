# Help me shape Asteru Motion 💜

A question, an example, or an honest reaction is useful to me. You don’t need to write code to help.

- **Have an idea?** [Tell me what video you’d like to make](https://github.com/asterustudio/asteru-motion-community/discussions/categories/ideas) and what’s difficult about it today.
- **Have a question?** [Ask in Q&A](https://github.com/asterustudio/asteru-motion-community/discussions/categories/q-a). English and Russian are both welcome.
- **Something isn’t working?** [Report it here](https://github.com/asterustudio/asteru-motion-community/issues/new/choose). A short description and your browser or device help me understand it.
- **Spotted a typo or a broken link?** Tell me, or suggest a small text correction in a pull request.

For the beta, please use the [waitlist form](https://asteru-motion.asterustudio.workers.dev/#join). For a free promo video or a private question, [message me on Telegram](https://t.me/daniarjabagin).

Please share only material you’re comfortable making public. Email addresses, passwords, and private screenshots belong in a private conversation.

Thanks for helping me make this better 🙂
